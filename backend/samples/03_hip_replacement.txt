BOWEL CARE

WOUND CARE
Keep the dressing clean and dry. You may shower on 06/20 (7 days); no baths, pools or hot tubs
for 6 weeks. Do not put creams or ointments on the incision. Stitches/staples are removed at the
2-week visit.

ACTIVITY (hip precautions for 6 weeks)
- Do NOT bend your hip past 90 degrees (no low chairs or toilets without a raised seat).
- Do NOT cross your legs or ankles.
- Do NOT twist your body toward the operated leg.
- Walk with the walker as taught by physical therapy; short walks 4-5 times a day.
- No driving until cleared at your follow-up visit.
